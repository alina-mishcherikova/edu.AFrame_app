🎨 XR Mini Exhibit

Explore artifacts in augmented reality. During the experience, you can move around the exhibit and interact with the objects. You have 2 differents modes: visit and edit. In visit mode, you can explore the exhibit and interact with the objects. In edit mode, you can move, rotate and scale the objects to customize your exhibit. To switch between modes, simply press the grip button on your controller. Press START to begin your journey.
